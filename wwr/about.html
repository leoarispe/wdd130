About Us - SPLASH White Water Rafting

We are passionate about adventure, nature, and unforgettable experiences. Our mission is to bring people closer to the excitement of White Water Rafting, offering opportunities to explore beautiful rivers, challenge themselves, and enjoy amazing moments with friends and family. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced adventure, SPLASH invites you to discover the thrill of rafting, enjoy the pwower of the nature, and create memories that will endure in the long term.

History

SPLASH White Water Rafting was founded in 1996 by a team of passionate rafters and outdoor adventure enthusiasts who share a dream aimed to introduce more people to the excitement of White Water Rafting. Inspired by the beautiful rivers and challenging rapids of Argentina, particularly the Atuel River in Mendoza, our founders decided to create a place where adventure, teamwork, and a love for nature could come together. Over the years, that original dream has grown into a mission to inspire new generations of adventures. Today, we continue to believe that every river has a story to tell and every journey is an opportunity to experience something extraordinary.
