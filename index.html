Buenos Aires

Buenos Aires is known for its highly visited landmarks, such as the Puente de la Mujer and the Obelisco. Furthermore, the restaurants play a prominent role; the famous Bodegones serve traditional dishes like Asado, Milanesa, and other traditional specialties. Buenos Aires is also recognized for its ecological reserves, which are located throughout the province. Within the city itself, the Palermo neighborhood is a favorite among both tourists and residents. A particular place is the Bosques de Palermo, located near the Japanese Garden and the Botanical Garden. This area features a large circuit surrounding a lake, where people enjoy a variety of recreational activities. Nearby, there is a wooded area filled with diverse plant life that includes roses, as well as animals like ducks and geese, along with pathways perfect for enjoying beautiful moments accompanied by the symphony of nature.
